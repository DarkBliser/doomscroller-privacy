Privacy Policy

Doomscroller (Android app com.darkbliser.doomscroller and Windows version) · Effective: 12 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Hannes Mähne
Contact: via the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play page.

2. Summary

  • No account, no advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no in-app purchases.
  • We (the developer) do not run any servers and do not receive or store your personal data.
  • Save games and settings stay on your device.
  • Only if you play online with a join code, Unity's game services process the data needed to connect you with the other players (see section 5).

3. Data stored only on your device

The game saves your characters, progress, coins, items and your settings (volume, language, controls, player name) on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Singleplayer and local multiplayer (Join IP)

In singleplayer no data leaves your device. In local multiplayer (Host LAN / Join IP) your device exchanges game data directly with the devices of the other players in the same network: your player name, character, positions, actions and the coins and items of the session. The host's local IP address is shown in the lobby so that friends can join. No internet server is involved.

5. Online multiplayer (join code)

Online play uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Sessions/Lobby, Relay) of Unity Technologies (Unity Technologies ApS, Denmark, and Unity Software Inc., USA). Only when you host or join a game with a join code, the following data is processed:

  • an anonymous player ID, created by Unity on your first online game and stored on your device (it is not linked to your name, email or phone number),
  • your IP address and technical connection data (needed to connect the devices),
  • your player name and the game data of the session (character, positions, actions), which are passed on to the other players of your game,
  • the join code and session information, and short latency measurements to choose the nearest server region,
  • technical diagnostic data about the operation of Unity's services.

Purpose and legal basis: providing the online multiplayer you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and the technical security and stability of the connection (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If the players are in the same Wi-Fi, the game switches to a direct connection between the devices when the game starts.

Unity may process data in the USA. Unity uses appropriate safeguards for this (such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or EU Standard Contractual Clauses). We do not receive this data. Storage periods follow Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Google Play

When you download the app from Google Play, Google processes data according to its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for multiplayer.
  • Wake lock: keeps Wi-Fi in low-latency mode while a multiplayer game is running (lower ping). It is released when you leave the game.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

8. Children

The game does not ask for any personal information. Apart from the online multiplayer data described above, no data is collected – also not from children.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Because we do not store your data, please contact us and we will help you with requests concerning Unity's services. You can delete the anonymous player ID on your device at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the game changes. The current version is always available on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: please use the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play page.
